# Active 3D assets / provenance

Provider: Innerscene 3D Parts Library. Each linked model page declares CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Retrieved 2026-10-22. The human model pages credit the MakeHuman/MPFB body, rig and skin sources as CC0 adaptations. No assets from The Sims are included.

These redistributed GLBs were optimized using glTF Transform 4.5.1: Meshopt geometry compression, WebP textures capped at 512 px, no simplification, no instancing on vehicle parts. Static palms and sofas are flattened and joined by material. Runtime vehicle batching preserves wheel pivots; human clones preserve their skeleton and Idle_Standing clip. Vehicle paint and clothing tint are adapted at runtime.
