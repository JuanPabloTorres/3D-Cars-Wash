Licenses: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/ and https://polyhaven.com/license

Base color uses sRGB; OpenGL normals and roughness are linear data. No metallic,
displacement, opacity or specular maps are needed. Texture density is per metre,
not stretched once across the whole lot. The shared original full-scene materials
are preserved. Ground054 was rejected as too sandy. Forest Ground 05 was tested
but its fine grain read too flat from the entrance; Brown Mud 03 replaces it.

The distant background remains the user-supplied `site-background.png`, 1672 × 941.
It is a perspective photograph, not a 360 panorama. Aspect-preserving cover may
crop it on mobile. No upscaling is represented as additional captured detail.
